04   /   Execution and limits

Quote motion is observable. Queue position is not.

Recorded-spread model

150 ms action delay; 1 tick penetration; $3.50/lot per side; $0.02/oz exit slip. A buy marks at bid, a sell at ask. All eligible working tickets can fill on the same quote.

Stress model

Both sides widened outward by 10% of spread; 450 ms delay; 50 ticks penetration; $5/lot per side; $0.06/oz exit slip. Not a worst-case bound.

Five limits are intended, not guaranteed to be sent or filled. Quote-change activity is not traded volume. Ordering, reversal, spread and latency can turn a $2 exit intent into less or even a loss. No exchange queue, real fills or native broker orders were observed.

Sessions use local DST clocks, exclude rollover, weekends and warm-up; this is not a 24/7 or multi-instrument result. One basket, up to 20 attempts/day and four per session/day. Initial modeled risk ≤$5, daily gate ≤$20 on $1,000; stops can gap.
