# What is measured, and what is not

## Market microstructure and execution

* **Activity is not volume or genuine order-flow imbalance.** Cont, Kukanov & Stoikov studied changes in best bid/ask *prices and sizes* on equities: [The Price Impact of Order Book Events](https://arxiv.org/abs/1011.6402). The Exness quote stream used here has no comparable queue or size history. PulseFive counts observed price changes and measures directional efficiency; neither proves buying/selling pressure.
* **A touch is not a fill.** State, distance, queue position and event rates enter order-book fill models: Lokin & Yu, [Fill Probabilities in a Limit Order Book](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.02572). Applying an equity/FX queue probability to this OTC gold feed would be unjustified. PulseFive instead discloses two simple penetration/delay assumptions. Passive entry can miss a move that continues and acquire inventory precisely when price reverses. Equal penetration of several eligible legs can fill them all in the model (unknown available size); fills are not broker receipts.
* **Quote data.** [Exness tick history](https://www.exness.com/tick-history/) and [bid/ask archive guide](https://www.exness.com/blog/product/exness-tick-history-bid-ask-prices/). Exact `XAUUSDm` archive URLs/digests are in `research/sources.json`. The archive clock is UTC; ties preserve file order. New prices can fill/stop working paper tickets inside a sampled poll. Entry decisions use only information received by the observation boundary. A buy enters vs ask and closes vs bid; a sell does the reverse. Spread is not debited a second time.
* **Order lifecycles.** [MT5 OrderSend](https://www.mql5.com/en/docs/trading/ordersend), [order properties](https://www.mql5.com/en/docs/constants/tradingconstants/orderproperties) and [deal properties](https://www.mql5.com/en/docs/constants/tradingconstants/dealproperties) distinguish requests, accepted orders, executions and position identifiers. A partial use of one 0.01-lot ticket is not inferred from historical quotes. Partial-lot, delayed-deal and cancel/fill reconciliation are exercised with **mock receipts only**. `terminal.py` retains a quarantined demo receipt/preflight reference; its native mutation method always refuses. It is not the forward runner.
* **Live observation.** MT5 [copy_ticks_range](https://www.mql5.com/en/docs/python_metatrader5/mt5copyticksrange_py) documents UTC datetimes, bid/ask versus trade flags, and `None` on error. The forward client requests price-info history, verifies the already-consumed same-millisecond prefix and uses receipt wall time for decisions. It does not assume `symbol_info_tick` polling captures every move. In contrast to historical replay, terminal history visibility, scheduling and missing/delayed events are not controllable. Do not treat forward paper as native broker fills.

## Sessions and statistical controls

Local wall-clock weekday windows are Tokyo 09–17, London 08–17, New York 08–17 and Sydney 08–17. Overlaps are counted once in this priority: London + New York, London alone, New York alone, Tokyo, Sydney. The London/Asia overlap goes to London. NY 16:45–18:15, UTC weekends and 00:00–00:25 / 23:58–24:00 UTC are excluded. A gap of >10 s invalidates the baseline; allow the full 20 prior complete observed M1 bars again. These are research labels, not broker holiday/news hours. DST uses the local IANA clocks. During the November test, London–NY overlap was 13:00–17:00 UTC (16:00–20:00 East Africa time).

For each eligible edge the first-stage ranker uses range ratio, quote pace, directional efficiency, micro-move/spread, spread/base range, base range, elapsed fraction of the minute and direction. The ridge penalty is fixed at 20. Compare p50/p80/p95 of fit-period scores and shallow/patient limit offsets. A high percentile is a **relative rank**; thresholds may be negative and are not required to imply positive expected dollars. The one-sided validation screen uses mean weekday P&L minus 2.65 standard errors plus net/PF/fill-count/day requirements under BOTH cost cases. This is a rejection heuristic, not a multiple-testing-adjusted prediction interval. September confirmation vetoes weak outcomes. No ex-post per-session approvals.

Base: recorded spreads; 150 ms placement/withdrawal/exit delay; $0.001/oz penetration; $3.50/lot/side fee; exit slip $0.02/oz. Stress: widen each side by 10% of the original spread, outward-rounded; 450 ms, $0.050/oz penetration; $5/lot/side, $0.06/oz exit slip. The *decision* estimates exit value with the same baseline allowances even under stress, while *booked* cash uses scenario costs. Weekly funding, venue liquidity, queue priority, broker rejections and outages are not calibrated. No swap modeled; the strategy is intended to hold seconds. These fill models are sensitivities, not a high-confidence worst case. Coarser latency, minimum lot changes, news gaps, market impact or outages can be worse.
