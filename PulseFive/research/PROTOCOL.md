# Frozen evaluation protocol

Before running any P&L evaluation: use the source defaults in `policy.py`; do not optimize on the report. One independent $1,000 USD account per period/scenario, 1:100 modeled gold leverage, 5 × 0.01 lot, 100 oz/lot. October 1–16 is the engineering sample; October 19–30 is the chronological evaluation sample. The latter dates have featured in an earlier, unrelated Hull study: this is a forward time split for this strategy, not genuinely unseen market data for the researcher. The archive contains 5,914,197 quotes. Each weekday reads from 11:37 UTC for warmup; new entries 12:00–16:00 UTC. Stop processing only after the daily limit when flat. No cherry-picked “best candle”, no cash replenishment inside a run.

Base: recorded spread; sampled 50 ms clock; 150 ms placement/cancel/market-exit latency; one tick of penetration beyond a limit; fill at limit without improvement. Commission $3.50/lot/side, modeled market-exit slippage $0.02/oz. Stress: same entry policy and spread cap, widen each side by 10% of the original spread rounded OUT per millidollar, 450 ms latency, $0.050/oz penetration, $5/lot/side, $0.06/oz exit slippage. No fill-size/queue data: a penetrating quote fills an eligible full 0.01-lot ticket; different tickets can fill separately, orders can fill during a pending cancel. This is an assumption, not a guarantee. Hard stops see original quote events (stressed quotes in stress run); price gaps can exceed planned losses. No added swap during these hours. Report all attempts, all costs and equity drawdown, not just wins. No news feed, market-impact/depth model or broker outage model. Parameters are hypotheses, not paper-derived optimal thresholds.
