# Session training protocol • fixed before trial outcomes

Goal: learn which gold momentum/pullback attempts, if any, justify a $2 basket target after execution costs. Not optimization until a backtest turns green. Do not retrain on the final test.

* Instrument: Exness XAUUSDm. June–July 2026: fit. August: choose a policy per session and apply a cost/evidence hurdle. September: separate chronological confirmation; can veto, never promote or refit. October is excluded from model selection/test: earlier work already inspected it. November 2–17: sealed final comparison. No later quotes admitted.
* Nonoverlapping session labels with local weekday/DST clocks: London+New York overlap, London only, New York only, Tokyo/Asia, Sydney/Pacific (in this priority). Do not trade the NY 16:45–18:15 rollover block, disjoint closed periods, stale data or unsupported symbols. These are research windows, not an exchange calendar.
* Fixed entry signal: high-volatility forming M1 candle, contemporaneous directional efficiency and observed quote-change activity; no traded-volume or order-book claims. First eligible fresh edge per UTC minute. All decisions use only data visible by a 50 ms observation. Earlier minutes warm each day. Label each candidate with the unchanged five-ticket engine under two predefined limit depths (shallow, patient). Future ticks are used ONLY for realized labels. Each hypothetical label starts independently at $1,000; they are not an equity curve.
* Fit per-session, per-depth standardized ridge (penalty 20) on *all attempts*, zero for unfilled, using entry-time features only. Compare three thresholds from the fit-period predictions (50th/80th/95th percentiles). Validation rank = larger of the candidates by worst (base/stress) mean daily net; ties by deterministic name. At most four attempts/session/UTC day in comparisons. Do not call the best losing candidate deployable.
* August promotion requires in EACH cost scenario >=20 filled baskets on >=8 dates, PF >=1.12, positive net and mean daily net minus 2.65 sample standard errors >0 (include eligible weekdays with zero trades). September confirmation of the chosen candidate requires positive net, PF>=1, >=10 fills on >=5 days in each scenario. Otherwise abstain. Fixed hurdles are conservative screens, not statistical guarantees.
* November comparisons: (1) unfiltered shallow baseline, (2) best research challenger for each session even if it failed, (3) approved-only release. Each full policy/scenario uses ONE chronological $1,000 account, 1:100 modeled leverage, one global basket, 20-attempt/day and 2%/day loss guard, 4 attempts/session/day. No resets between days within an account. Report session-attributed attempts/fills/P&L, profit factor, actual realizations >=$2, and marked drawdown. Zero trades != a profitable strategy.
* Base: recorded spread, $3.50/lot/side, 150 ms place/remove/exit delay, 1 tick penetration, $0.02/oz exit slip. Stress: both sides widened outward by 10% of spread, $5/lot/side, 450 ms delay, 50 ticks penetration, $0.06/oz exit slip. All original updates can fill or stop an order during its life, including while cancellation is pending. No depth/queue/fill receipts, no live-market connection here.

For consistent UTC-day ledgers, use Monday–Friday UTC only; warm with the first 25 minutes and disable entries after 23:58 UTC. This excludes the thin Sunday reopen. No claim for weekends, rollover or unobserved markets.
